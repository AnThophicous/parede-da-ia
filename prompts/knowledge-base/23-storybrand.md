<core_axioms>
## 1. Axioms

1. **The customer is the hero, the brand is the guide.** Sites that talk about themselves lose attention; sites that talk about the customer's problem win it.
2. **Narrative structure:** a **character** (the customer) **wants something**, faces a **problem** (external, internal and philosophical), meets a **guide** (the brand, with empathy + authority) who gives a **plan** (3 steps), **calls them to action**, helps them **avoid failure** and ends in **success**.
3. **Clarity beats creativity** in copy: if visitors have to burn calories to understand, they leave.
4. **One direct CTA** ("Book a demo") and one **transitional CTA** (a guide, a free assessment) for those who aren't ready.
5. **Repetition is an ally:** the same central promise appears in the hero, the middle and the end.
</core_axioms>

<page_blueprint>
## 2. A corporate landing blueprint

| Section | Question it answers | Content |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| Hero | What do you offer, to whom, and how do I start? | promise + literal subtitle + direct CTA + transitional CTA |
| Problem | Do you understand my pain? | 3 of the audience's pains in their own words |
| Guide | Why trust you? | empathy + authority (real numbers, authorized logos, certifications) |
| Plan | What happens next? | 3 simple steps |
| Proof | Does it work for people like me? | cases, real testimonials |
| Cost of inaction | What do I lose if I wait? | consequences (without alarmism) |
| Success | What does my life look like afterwards? | an image of the result |
| Final CTA | How do I start now? | the direct CTA repeated |
</page_blueprint>

<agent_traps>
## 3. Guard (forbidden)
- A company-centered hero ("We are leaders in…").
- Made-up numbers, testimonials or logos.
- More than one direct CTA competing in the same section.
- Exaggerated fear or manipulation.
</agent_traps>

## 4. Example
**Bad:** "Providing innovative solutions with excellence for 20 years."
**Good:** "Your clinic without spreadsheets: scheduling, records and billing in one place. Get started in 3 steps." → [Book a demo] · [See how it works in 2 min]
