<core_axioms>
## 1. Axioms

1. **A brand is a perception; an identity is what you control** (name, logo, color, type, voice, imagery, motion).
2. **Ideals of a strong identity:** vision, meaning, authenticity, differentiation, durability, coherence, flexibility, commitment and value.
3. **Process before aesthetics:** research → strategy → design → touchpoints → management. Skipping the research is designing in the dark.
4. **A brief is a contract:** goal, audience, positioning, competition, attributes, constraints, approvers and metrics.
5. **System > pieces:** the identity has to work at every touchpoint (site, social, slides, product, email).
6. **Consistency with flexibility:** clear rules (colors, spacing, logo usage) and controlled freedom (compositions, images).
7. **Motion and sound are brand too** in digital: a coherent motion language reinforces the identity `[13]`.
</core_axioms>

<web_translation>
## 2. Translating an identity to the web

| Element | Translation |
|---------|-------------|
| Brand color | tokens with tones generated in OKLCH; the brand color becomes an accent, not the background of everything (unless the direction is corporate-brand) |
| Typography | web license verified; a fallback with adjusted metrics; its own scale |
| Logo | optimized SVG, clear space respected, a monochrome version for complex backgrounds |
| Voice | microcopy for buttons, errors and empty states in the brand's tone |
| Imagery | consistent photo/illustration direction; AI only with approval and versioned prompts |
| Motion | motion adjectives derived from the brand's attributes |
</web_translation>

<agent_traps>
## 3. Guard (forbidden)
- Recreating or "redesigning" the client's logo without an explicit request.
- Using client/partner logos without permission.
- Imitating the identity of famous brands (legal and reputational risk).
- Choosing a color "because it's trendy" with no link to attributes and audience.
</agent_traps>

---

<review_checklist>
## 4. Rubric
- [ ] Every brand element used came from the client or is marked as a proposal.
- [ ] Color/type tokens derive from the manual (or from the approved proposal).
- [ ] The page is recognizable as the brand's even without the logo (test: cover the logo on the capture).
- [ ] Microcopy in the defined tone of voice.
</review_checklist>
